# Vector.ai (Raft)

> Vector.ai, now operating as Raft (raft.ai), is an AI-powered logistics automation
> platform for freight forwarders, customs brokers, and trade finance teams. The Raft
> Intelligence Engine combines organization data, contextual LLMs, orchestration, and
> specialized governed agents to automate document processing, invoice matching, data
> extraction, and exception handling across supply chain operations.

Raft publishes no public developer API, API reference, or SDKs. The platform is sold
as an enterprise application (app.raft.ai) that integrates with customers' TMS and
ERP systems. Their documentation portal hosts legal and compliance documents rather
than developer documentation.
